Safety

Handled with care, every cylinder, every time

Our team follows strict handling and storage practices from bulk delivery through to the cylinder you take home. If you ever smell gas, we're one call away.

Read our safety guidance

Smell gas?

Turn off the cylinder valve, ventilate the area, avoid switches, call us immediately.

Storage

Keep cylinders upright, outdoors or well-ventilated, away from heat.

Inspection

We check valves, hoses, and seals at every exchange.

Transport

Cylinders are always transported upright and secured.
